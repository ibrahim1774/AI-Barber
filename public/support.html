AI Barber Support

AI Barber builds, publishes and hosts a website for your barbershop, on the web at aibarber.org and in the AI Barber iPhone app.

Contact us

Email support@davoxa.com. We reply within 2 business days. If your question is about a published site, include the shop name or the site's web address.

Edit and republish your site

In the app, open the Edit tab, make your changes, then tap Publish. Your live site updates with the new version. Republishing is included in your subscription. Your site's live link is in the Settings tab.

Restore a purchase on a new phone

Make sure the phone is signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with, install AI Barber, and go to Settings → Restore purchases. The app finds your subscription and loads your site.

Cancel a subscription

  • Bought in the app: on your iPhone, open Settings → your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → AI Barber and tap Cancel Subscription. Apple handles app billing and refunds.
  • Bought on aibarber.org: email support@davoxa.com from the email you used at checkout and we will cancel it.

Cancelling stops future charges. It does not take your site offline by itself; ask us if you want it removed.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. Or email support@davoxa.com from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days. If you also want your published site taken down, say so in your email. See the Privacy Policy for details.
