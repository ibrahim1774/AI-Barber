Prime Barber Privacy Policy · Effective October 20, 2026

Privacy Policy

What Prime Barber collects, why, who helps us run it, and how to get your information removed.

Prime Barber builds and hosts websites for barbershops. It is run by Davoxa ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Prime Barber website at aibarber.org and the Prime Barber iOS app (together, "Prime Barber"). It does not cover the websites of the barbershops we host, other than what we describe below.

What we collect

  • Shop details. Information you type in, or that we import for you from a booking link (such as Booksy, theCut, Fresha, Square, Vagaro or StyleSeat) or from a Google Business listing: shop name, address or area, phone number, opening hours, services and prices, reviews, and photos.
  • Photos you upload to your site, such as a logo, haircut photos or photos of your shop.
  • Account details, if you create an account: your name, email address, phone number and password (passwords are handled by our login provider and we cannot read them).
  • Contact details you give us in a form, such as your name, email, phone number and shop name, along with the page you were on, the time, your IP address and your browser type.
  • Purchase and subscription status, such as which plan you bought and whether it is active. We do not receive your full card details.
  • Device and usage information, such as pages and screens viewed, buttons tapped, device and browser type, approximate location from your IP address, and, where you allow it, advertising identifiers. See "Analytics and advertising" below.

Your clients' information

If you use Prime Barber's booking or store features, your clients (the people who book or order from your site) enter personal information on your site's booking and shop pages: their name, phone number, email address, the service or products they choose, the time they want, any notes they add, and, if they pay by card, a payment confirmation (we never see their card details).

  • Who sees it. You, the shop owner, and the staff you add, see it in the Prime Barber app. We hold it on your behalf and use it only to provide the booking and store service: to show it to you, run your bookings and orders, and process payments.
  • How long we keep it. For as long as your site's booking or store features are on, so you keep your appointment and order history, and for as long as the law requires us to keep payment records. When you delete your account, we delete it. Taking your website offline on its own removes the site but keeps your clients' booking and order history until you delete your account.
  • Deletion requests. A client who wants their information removed can email support@davoxa.com or ask the shop. We will delete it within 30 days, unless we must keep a payment record.

Why we use it

  • To generate your barbershop website, host it, and let you edit and republish it.
  • To provide your account, process your subscription, and answer support requests.
  • To follow up when you ask about our service or start building a site.
  • To measure how Prime Barber is used, fix problems, improve the product, and measure and improve our advertising.

Where it lives

  • Supabase stores accounts, saved site data and your clients' bookings and orders.
  • Supabase Auth (our login provider) sends account emails such as password resets, from support@davoxa.com.
  • Google Cloud Storage stores uploaded and imported images and the files used to build your site.
  • Vercel hosts aibarber.org and the websites we publish for you. If you buy a custom domain through us, it is registered through Vercel.
  • On the website, a draft of your site may also be saved in your own browser so you don't lose your work.

Published websites are public by design. Anything you put on your published site, including shop details, photos and reviews, can be seen by anyone on the internet.

Importing your shop

When you paste a booking link or search for your Google Business listing, we fetch the public information on that page or listing so we can fill in your site. To do this we use Google's Places service and Apify, a web data service that reads the public booking page you point us to. We only use what we import to build your site.

Analytics and advertising

We use the following tools. Each receives information about how you use Prime Barber, and some help us measure whether our ads work.

  • On aibarber.org: Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API, TikTok Pixel and TikTok Events API, Microsoft Clarity (session recordings and heatmaps), and Triple Whale (marketing attribution). When you submit a form or make a purchase, we may share your email address or phone number with Meta and TikTok in hashed (scrambled) form so they can match the event to an ad.
  • On the websites we publish for you: Microsoft Clarity, so we can see how the sites perform.
  • In the iOS app: PostHog (product analytics), AppsFlyer (install and ad attribution), Superwall (to show plans) and RevenueCat (to record subscription status). On iOS, the app asks for your permission before using your device's advertising identifier. If you decline, we do not use it.

You can limit ad tracking in your device or browser settings, and you can use your browser's settings to block or clear cookies.

Payments and messages

  • In the iOS app, subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple ID.
  • On aibarber.org, payments are processed by Stripe.
  • Card payments from your clients are processed by Stripe Connect. Stripe collects your payout and identity details when you set up payouts, and your clients' card details when they pay. We receive the payment confirmation, not the card details.
  • Push notifications in the app (new bookings and orders, for example) are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple's Push Notification service, which receive your device's push token and the notification text.

We never see or store your full card details. We receive a confirmation of the payment, your plan, and the email address you used at checkout.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers named in this policy (hosting, storage, login, payments, push delivery, analytics and advertising measurement), and only what they need to do their job. Every service provider we share data with is bound by contract to protect it at least as well as this policy does and only to use it to provide services to us.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account or website is active, and as needed to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

  • In the app, go to Settings and choose Delete account. We take your published app website offline, delete its stored content and the photos uploaded for it, release any custom domain, and delete your login and the records saved to your account. Earlier versions of the page that our hosting provider (Vercel) may still serve under old deployment addresses are removed if you email us. Your App Store subscription is separate: Apple keeps billing you until you cancel it in your Apple ID settings.
  • By email, write to support@davoxa.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete your account and data. We will do so within 30 days.
  • Without an account, use Settings, then Take my website offline, in the app. We confirm your purchase with Apple and then take your app website offline and delete its stored content and photos. You can also email support@davoxa.com.
  • Cancelling your subscription does not delete your website by itself. If your subscription ends, we replace your site with an "unavailable" page and keep your content so you can come back; ask us if you want it deleted.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to how we use it. Email us and we will help.

Children

Prime Barber is a tool for businesses. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Prime Barber changes. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to let you know.
