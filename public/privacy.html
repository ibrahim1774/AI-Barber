AI Barber Privacy Policy · Effective October 20, 2026

Privacy Policy

What AI Barber collects, why, who helps us run it, and how to get your information removed.

AI Barber builds and hosts websites for barbershops. It is run by Davoxa ("we", "us"). This policy covers the AI Barber website at aibarber.org and the AI Barber iOS app (together, "AI Barber"). It does not cover the websites of the barbershops we host, other than what we describe below.

What we collect

  • Shop details. Information you type in, or that we import for you from a booking link (such as Booksy, theCut, Fresha, Square, Vagaro or StyleSeat) or from a Google Business listing: shop name, address or area, phone number, opening hours, services and prices, reviews, and photos.
  • Photos you upload to your site, such as a logo, haircut photos or photos of your shop.
  • Account details, if you create an account: your name, email address, phone number and password (passwords are handled by our login provider and we cannot read them).
  • Contact details you give us in a form, such as your name, email, phone number and shop name, along with the page you were on, the time, your IP address and your browser type.
  • Purchase and subscription status, such as which plan you bought and whether it is active. We do not receive your full card details.
  • Device and usage information, such as pages and screens viewed, buttons tapped, device and browser type, approximate location from your IP address, and, where you allow it, advertising identifiers. See "Analytics and advertising" below.

Why we use it

  • To generate your barbershop website, host it, and let you edit and republish it.
  • To provide your account, process your subscription, and answer support requests.
  • To follow up when you ask about our service or start building a site.
  • To measure how AI Barber is used, fix problems, improve the product, and measure and improve our advertising.

We do not sell your personal information.

Where it lives

  • Supabase stores accounts and saved site data.
  • Google Cloud Storage stores uploaded and imported images and the files used to build your site.
  • Vercel hosts aibarber.org and the websites we publish for you. If you buy a custom domain through us, it is registered through Vercel.
  • On the website, a draft of your site may also be saved in your own browser so you don't lose your work.

Published websites are public by design. Anything you put on your published site, including shop details, photos and reviews, can be seen by anyone on the internet.

Importing your shop

When you paste a booking link or search for your Google Business listing, we fetch the public information on that page or listing so we can fill in your site. To do this we use Google's Places service and Apify, a web data service that reads the public booking page you point us to. We only use what we import to build your site.

Analytics and advertising

We use the following tools. Each receives information about how you use AI Barber, and some help us measure whether our ads work.

  • On aibarber.org: Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API, TikTok Pixel and TikTok Events API, Microsoft Clarity (session recordings and heatmaps), and Triple Whale (marketing attribution). When you submit a form or make a purchase, we may share your email address or phone number with Meta and TikTok in hashed (scrambled) form so they can match the event to an ad.
  • On the websites we publish for you: Microsoft Clarity, so we can see how the sites perform.
  • In the iOS app: PostHog (product analytics), AppsFlyer (install and ad attribution) and Superwall (to show plans and record subscription status). On iOS, the app asks for your permission before using your device's advertising identifier. If you decline, we do not use it.

You can limit ad tracking in your device or browser settings, and you can use your browser's settings to block or clear cookies.

Payments

  • In the iOS app, subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple ID.
  • On aibarber.org, payments are processed by Stripe.

We never see or store your full card details. We receive a confirmation of the payment, your plan, and the email address you used at checkout.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account or website is active, and as needed to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

  • In the app, you can delete your account. This removes your login and the site records saved to your account.
  • By email, write to support@davoxa.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete your account and data. We will do so within 30 days.
  • Published websites stay online until you ask us to take them down. Deleting your account or cancelling your subscription does not take your site offline by itself. To take a site down and remove its photos, email support@davoxa.com and tell us which site, and we will remove it within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to how we use it. Email us and we will help.

Children

AI Barber is a tool for businesses. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as AI Barber changes. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to let you know.
